# Privacy Policy – Lakitu

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Lakitu Discord bot ("Lakitu", "the bot", "we") collects, how it is used and stored, and what rights you have.

## 1. Data We Collect

**Time-trial data (stored):**
When you use `/save`, we store:
- Your Discord user ID
- Track
- Mode (Shrooms / No Items)
- Your submitted time

**Server member data (not stored):**
Lakitu uses the Guild Members intent. Member information (member lists, role membership, display names, avatars) is only processed in memory while a command runs. We use it to show leaderboards for the current server or a selected role and to display player names. This data is never written to a database or log.

**What we do NOT collect:**
- Message content (Lakitu uses slash commands only)
- Presence or activity data
- IP addresses, emails or other personal information

## 2. How We Use Data

Data is used only to provide the bot's features:
- Saving, displaying and deleting personal best times
- Server and role leaderboards
- Statistics compared to world records
- Premium features for supporters

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics or AI training.

## 3. Storage and Security

Time-trial data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by **Supabase** (supabase.com), a third-party database provider that acts as our data processor. Supabase encrypts data in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted to the bot owner.

## 4. Third Parties

We don't sell, rent or share your personal data. The only third parties involved are:
- **Discord** – the platform the bot runs on
- **Supabase** – database hosting (see above)
- **MKCentral / Lounge API** – public player stats are requested from it. Your Discord user ID is only sent to look up premium members' Lounge profiles.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your times are stored until you delete them. You can delete any saved time at any time with `/delete`. To have all your data deleted, contact the owner (see below). We will remove it within 30 days.

If the bot is removed from a server, your saved times are not deleted, because they are linked to your user ID, not to a server.

## 6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your data, and to object to its processing. The legal basis for processing is our legitimate interest in providing the features you request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and, for premium, the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

To exercise these rights, contact us (see below). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Austria: Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).

## 7. Children

Lakitu is not directed at children under 13. Like Discord, we require users to meet Discord's minimum age. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be published here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.
